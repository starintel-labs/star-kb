* Inference

A useful non-LLM autonomous system is a closed loop of =observe -> derive -> propose -> prioritize -> act -> evaluate -> learn -> review/promote=. It can improve from recorded outcomes without modifying its own approval policy. Two distinct products emerge: (1) an auditable learner producing candidate rules and candidate facts; (2) a constrained planner selecting permitted actor actions from a fixed capability registry. Neither owns canonical truth.

The best first reward objective is *independently verified new links per resource consumed*, not raw count of relation documents. Merely asserting a link must earn zero positive reward. Likewise, missing evidence is not a negative example in an open-world corpus.

* Operator-toggleable maximizers

A maximizer is a versioned *operational policy profile*, not an arbitrary Prolog predicate passed to a service or a StarIntel document-field extension. Every profile has =profile_id=, =revision=, =enabled objectives=, =finite nonnegative weights=, =normalization definition=, =policy/eligibility constraints=, =resource budget=, and =operator provenance=. Old runs retain their original profile revision.

| Candidate maximizer | Positive outcome measured | Anti-gaming condition |
|---------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------|
| verified-links | Independent, verified novel subject-predicate-object relationship | Unique normalized edge fingerprint and independent evidence; candidate creation alone scores zero |
| validated-facts | Promoted, supported novel fact | Distinct claim fingerprint and successful canonical-ingest receipt |
| contradiction-resolution | Explicitly closed contradictory evidence with trace | Preserve contested evidence; do not erase/relabel inconvenient observations |
| missing-evidence-coverage | Materially reduced missing evidence for approved targets | Measure against a fixed requirement snapshot, not self-defined easy targets |
| information-gain | Reduced uncertainty or improved held-out predictive loss | Use calibration and counterfactual baseline; avoid counting confident but false predictions |
| source-diversity | Added independent evidence family with provenance | Multiple reposts of one source count as one evidence family |
| efficient-execution | Accepted outcome per bounded work unit | Track wall time, paid calls, CPU, tokens=0 for no-LLM mode, and resource budget |

Hard constraints (=schema validity=, =dataset/capability scope=, =provenance=, =privacy=, =independent human review when mandated=, =actor admission=, =budget=) are not rewards and cannot be disabled by sliders. A link between persons does not imply collaboration, endorsement, intent, or guilt. Unknown/ambiguous identities must remain separate candidates.

Proposed score over eligible actions =a= and enabled objectives =i=:

#+begin_src text
maximize_a  sum_i [ normalized_weight_i *
                    expected(validated_gain_i(a) - baseline_i) ]
            - cost(a) - expected_harm_penalty(a)

subject to:
  authorized(a) AND schema_compatible(a) AND within_budget(a)
  AND no_unsupported_promotion(a) AND tenant_and_dataset_scope(a)
#+end_src

For selecting a *set* of actions, use binary decision variables with explicit compute, latency, call, and concurrency budgets. Normalize all objective units before combining; do not add incomparable counts, seconds, and percentages directly. Define each reward at the *outcome event*, not at proposal emission. Negative outcomes, later refutations, and reversals post compensating reward-accounting events rather than silently editing history. Use deterministic tie-breaking and snapshot the candidate set, random seed, model/solver version, objective revision and outcome cutoff.

* Aleph learning protocol

1. Build *background predicates* from the pinned canonical projection, immutable source/evidence snapshots, and approved existing rules. Never let a learner invent source facts or import untrusted clauses into the privileged Prolog process.
2. Use explicit positive/negative *reviewed labels* as examples. Unknown cases are unlabelled, not negative. Track reviewer identity, evidence cutoff, reason, provenance, and subsequent correction.
3. Define head/body modes, determinations, allowed term types, clause-depth/length limits, timeouts and sample budgets. Train the Aleph subprocess on a fixed manifest and isolated workspace.
4. Produce a *candidate clause*, support and counterexamples, precision/recall, calibration where applicable, held-out source/time-split performance, complexity and proof tree. Historical links must not leak across entity or source families into validation examples.
5. Never consult generated clauses directly in server authority contexts. Review, lint, test, stage and approve a versioned module in =star-kb=; invalidate dependent conclusions when an adopted rule is later superseded.

Example *hypothesis shape only* (not an approved rule):

#+begin_src prolog
needs_review(Entity) :-
    contradictory_evidence(Entity),
    outdated_primary_source(Entity),
    missing_independent_source(Entity).
#+end_src

Aleph can learn logic patterns but it does not optimize arbitrary business outcomes on its own. Reward optimization belongs to a separate planner; reward-derived labels must not become unreviewed truth.

* Active learning and human feedback

Choose label requests from eligible unresolved candidates using uncertainty, expected error reduction, diversity, review cost, and topic/tenant scope. A proposed first policy is =expected_information_gain / estimated_review_cost=, with a queue fairness limit and exploration floor. A simple uncertainty sampling baseline is mandatory for comparison; label count reduction alone is not proof of improvement.

A reviewer can label =supported=, =refuted=, =insufficient-evidence=, =identity-ambiguous=, =not-relevant=, or =requires-more-evidence=. Persist event-time reason and the evidence seen by the reviewer. Separate a factual label from an operator preference about investigation usefulness. Feedback is versioned and can be corrected; historical records stay auditable.

For an optional strategy-selection learner, compare a deterministic greedy baseline, a budgeted integer optimizer, and a seeded multi-armed bandit (e.g. Beta-Bernoulli Thompson sampling for binary verified outcomes). Nonstationary data and delayed reward require temporal evaluation; do not treat all edges/actors as independent identical trials. Bound exploration and avoid feedback loops that repeatedly sample the same sources.

* Candidate fact and canonical document path

| Stage | Artifact | Owner | Requirement |
|-------+----------+-------+-------------|
| Inferred | Prolog result plus derivation tree and source IDs | Star KB | Replayable snapshot; clearly marked derived |
| Proposed | Candidate fact/link payload and normalized fingerprint | Learning actor / PR #8 event log | No authority; idempotent identity |
| Reviewed | Human/independent votes and counterevidence | PR #8 review/vote protocol | Reviewer independence and full history |
| Verified | Formal admission certificate | PR #8 Prolog verifier | Exact schema, provenance, source/evidence and spec digest checks |
| Materialized | Canonical StarIntel document and/or relation | Proposed starintel-prolog bridge | Build against pinned current manifest, no guessed dtype/fields |
| Ingested | Durable server receipt plus replay cursor | StarIntel Server | Re-read canonical revision; reject duplicates and stale writes |
| Rewarded | Outcome/reward ledger event | Planner/evaluator | Credit only the verified novelty and independently observed effect |

A fact can be both new and *wrong*. Candidate documents may be created for review; verified/published canonical facts must pass the full applicable gate. Do not invent a new =dtype=, =status= value, schema extension or custom field on a hunch. If a canonical contract change is required, it must be a *versioned StarIntel specification change* handled at the canonical schema authority, never hidden in extension or runtime policy. A new StarLang actor/message protocol likewise requires its own explicit protocol revision.

Materialization should generate stable IDs from canonical claim fingerprint plus source and tenant scopes; attach evidence/source IDs, derivation rule+digest, original document revisions, origin actor, run ID, timestamps and contested/refuted status using *actual approved schema fields*. Publication must occur through discovered contracted client operations, not direct privileged CouchDB access or a legacy endpoint. Issue a bounded target/operation to Star Server and let the existing idempotency, outbox, lease, capability and fencing behavior protect the write. Revisions that refute a fact produce correction/supersession events and revoke outstanding reward credit.

* Wolfram-StarIntel mathematical adapter

The proposed Wolfram integration is an *optional numerical/symbolic compute worker*, not a second knowledge store, policy engine or LLM. Fixed operations may include =optimize_action_batch= (mixed-integer), =compare_objectives= (Pareto frontier), =estimate_information_gain=, =fit_calibration= and =graph_statistics=. Callers send typed, bounded numeric features and a fixed operation identifier. The worker enforces argument shape, variable counts, CPU/time quotas and budget before translating into Wolfram Language; never accept arbitrary Wolfram expressions through an actor API.

A constrained optimizer can use =Maximize= or =NMaximize= on small problems. An entirely local deterministic solver must remain a supported fallback when Wolfram is not licensed, reachable, or permitted for data-classification reasons. No private corpus, individual-level personal data or secrets are ever transmitted to a Wolfram cloud service by default. Server command/control still validates the resulting action list.

Reproducible observed arithmetic fixture (Wolfram evaluator, 2026-10-10):

#+begin_src text
actions a,b,c: costs 3,4,2; rewards 5,7,4; capacity 5
binary maximize: 5*a + 7*b + 4*c
solver outcome: score 9, a=1, b=0, c=1
#+end_src

The reward fixture is *synthetic*. It tests numerical feasibility, not actual accuracy, threat prediction, or live investigator utility.

* Adversarial test matrix for future implementation

| Test | Expected result |
|------+-----------------|
| One actor proposes 10,000 near-identical links | One normalized candidate; no positive reward before verification |
| Ten reposts of the same claim | One evidence family; no independent corroboration |
| Ground truth later refutes a previously rewarded link | Append negative adjustment and invalidation; preserve audit history |
| Human labels a case unknown | Do not mint an Aleph negative example |
| Proposed rule succeeds on training but fails held-out time/source split | Reject activation, retain candidate for inspection |
| Wolfram adapter crashes/times out | No unauthorized action; deterministic fallback or typed failure |
| Optimizer selects out-of-scope target | Reject despite high reward score |
| Reward profile changes during action execution | Outcome attributed to original immutable profile revision |
| Evidence expires or source is retracted | Derived claims become stale/contested; schedule re-review |
| Document projection base/release/spec lock mismatch | No publication; typed version-blocker with evidence |
| User disables every soft maximizer | No autonomous positive-gain selection; hard safety/maintenance rules remain |
| Two proposed inferred edges point to ambiguous identities | Do not force merge, do not reward unverified association |
| Replaying identical events/snapshots | Same candidates, learning examples, selection and reward ledger |

Future acceptance criteria: full no-LLM offline fixture run; one Aleph positive/negative example set yields a held-out candidate rule; one human correction updates the next labeled-learning batch; toggles change action selection reproducibly; an inference yields a candidate canonical document; publication is refused without admission and schema validation; after authorized publish and server re-read a reward event is emitted once. Inspectable proof and terminal failure receipts are required.

* Contradictions

- PR #8 presents approved actor learning/admission code but remains open and unmergeable against current =main=. Reuse is contingent on rebase/verification, not on its prose alone.
- Raw link-count maximization conflicts with provenance and identity discipline. Proposed resolution: reward only evidence-verified *novel* links, deduplicate by semantics and source family, penalize corrections.
- Aleph induction is not the same as numeric reinforcement learning. The two need explicit example/feedback and reward ledgers and must not silently translate an optimization score into a truth claim.
- The system has an active StarIntel release dimension separate from immutable schema version, and PR #8 has a separate StarLang learning spec identity. All three must be pinned; apparent version discrepancies must be resolved against their authorities.
- The desire to create StarIntel facts conflicts with prohibiting KB projection writes. Resolution: candidate hypotheses can be staged; *only the scoped canonical publisher* may author/ingest validated documents.

These are research/design gates. They are not permission to modify the canonical StarIntel schema, activate learned rules, publish claims, crawl private sources, or begin bulk ingestion.

* Footnotes and Glossary

- *Aleph / ILP* — inductive logic programming: propose logical clauses from constrained background facts and explicitly labeled examples.
- *Active learning* — choose which unlabeled cases should be reviewed to improve a model efficiently.
- *Maximizer* — a weighted objective exposed to an operator, distinct from mandatory authorization/safety constraints.
- *Goodhart effect* — turning a proxy metric into an optimized target can degrade the real objective.
- *Reward ledger* — append-only attribution of verified outcomes, corrections, and their triggering profile revision.
- *Canonical StarIntel JSON* — immutable/versioned authoritative document records validated by StarIntel's pinned contract; a Prolog projection is derived.
- *StarLang learning spec* — proposed/PR #8 protocol identity for candidate/vote/audit events; not the same as the canonical StarIntel document specification.
